Motor Melee

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Motor Melee is an iOS app that lets you compare cars through animated, head-to-head battles. We believe your experience should be simple, fun, and private.

Data Collection and Storage

Motor Melee does not collect personal information. The app does not require an account or access to personal data on your device.

Vehicle information used for comparisons is included with the app. Your car selections and battle results are processed locally on your device and are not transmitted to external servers.

Any app preferences saved to support functionality remain on your device.

Analytics and Tracking

Motor Melee does not contain advertisements, third-party analytics, or tracking software.

We do not track your activity or sell or share your personal information with third parties.

External Links

Motor Melee includes an optional link to an external website for vehicle data attribution. Opening this link takes you outside the app, where the website's own privacy practices apply.

Your Privacy Choices

Motor Melee does not require an account or personal information to use the app.

You can remove locally stored app data by deleting Motor Melee from your device. Some data may remain in existing device backups.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. Any updates will be reflected on this page.
